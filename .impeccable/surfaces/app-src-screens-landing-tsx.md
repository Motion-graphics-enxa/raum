## Scope

Landing page `/` (Persuade, desktop 1440 first, phone 390), in the established Altbau Daylight world. Today's room picker moves to `/start`. Audience: the hackathon jury at the pitch, on the stage screen and on their phones. Frontend only. User: no Arabic anywhere on the page; examples are German into English and Russian. User wants a better story than "Amira's son", and maximum spectacle.

## Shape brief

1. **Job.** In seconds the jury knows what RAUM is (a private interpreter that lives in the room), why it is different (it checks every number, date and dose; nothing leaves the box; it forgets), and where it is used (three doors). Action: Open a room (to `/start`).
2. **Story.** Three doors in a Berlin corridor, behind each a conversation where a misheard number is a legal problem. Today the interpreter is a relative, a phone app, or nobody. Open a door: the room's sentence is translated and checked. Then one wrong number caught live (13 ≠ 12), seven models on one box with zero bytes out (pull the cable, it keeps working), and the summary that burns itself.
3. **Proof.** Only verified facts from PRODUCT.md; demo sentences labelled as examples; hardware named ASUS Ascent GX10.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the Berlin stairwell landing outside three practice doors; you choose a door and the room behind it opens into daylight. It refuses the AI-SaaS hero (headline, subcopy, button, row of cards) and the dark glowing AI page.

OWN-WORLD: Altbau Daylight as recorded in DESIGN.md: limewash wall, room paints below a 4 px walnut Begleitstrich, enamel door plates in Sofia Sans Extra Condensed caps, Sofia Sans text, daylight patches, pens green / amber / red, one depth rule.

STORY: The visitor reads "What's said in the room stays in the room", opens a door, watches a sentence being checked and a wrong date caught, pulls the internet cable and sees it keep working, watches the summary burn, then opens a room.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440×900. Nav on the wall (RAUM wordmark, How it checks, The rooms, Privacy, Open a room button). Headline at ~110 px condensed caps over a one-line offer. Three tall doors standing on a full-width slate dado (Sprechzimmer reseda, Kanzlei oxblood, Verhandlungsraum cobalt) with enamel plates, brass knobs, "Enter" on each paint; a slow daylight patch drifts across the wall.

FORM: Three doors in a corridor, an in-world structure the user picked over the dealt hand of the surface roll (seed:347c9b5eaab7bc5a, persuade). Signature interaction: the door swings open in 3D on its hinge and daylight floods out with that room's checked sentence; plus Lichtfall on the live check demo.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
